# WaveGate: Correct Pullbacks Before More Clients

Date: 2026-10-02. Frozen source: `351f27192b2280a780315ca336e2c2d5269d229d`.
This is correctness and bounded trainability evidence, **not** a speed comparison
or a language-model quality benchmark.

## Reproduced And Repaired

The effective gate is itself rewritten by porous saturation before the forward
affine operation. Its derivative was missing from the parameter pullback. In the
two-row regression fixture, the old analytic gate gradient was `+0.023985479`,
while finite difference gave `-0.0006646663`: even the sign was wrong. The repaired
analytic result is `-0.0006644177`, versus `-0.0006641448` from finite difference
on the final, scale-stabilized forward implementation.

`WaveGate::vjp` now returns input/gate/bias derivatives without averaging,
saturation of parameter gradients, or accumulation. Normal `Module::backward`
retains its explicit reduction/safety policy after applying the corrected chain
rule. Python exposes this on the existing native NN layer. Invalid pullbacks
are rejected before changing either accumulator.

Further scale checks found a curvature-insensitive near-zero derivative branch,
raw norm-squared overflow/underflow, and loss of shader precision near zero.
Shared CPU/WGPU projection arithmetic now handles those cases. The direct
projection of `[1e-30, 2e-30]` at curvature `-1e-30` is `[1e-15, 2e-15]` on both
tested paths; `[1e20, -2e20]` remains nonzero and normalized. An intermediate
shader candidate returned zero for the tiny pair; the final small-argument ratio
form fixes that without relaxing the comparison tolerance. Porous saturation's
forward/slope also use scale-safe ratios.
